# Pantry-Led Planning

Pantry-led cooking in this repo does not mean cooking only from shelf-stable
ingredients. It means using the pantry as the structure for weekly recipe
selection, then buying the fresh or specialty items that make the chosen dishes
worth cooking.

## Core Loop

1. Pick one or two pantry strengths for the week.
2. Choose recipes or candidates that use those strengths.
3. List what is already stock.
4. List on-demand perishables needed.
5. List recipe-specific non-perishables separately.
6. Cook and log what actually happened.
7. Promote repeated successes into stable house recipes.

## Ingredient Classes

### Stock

Durable ingredients normally expected to be available:

- grains,
- pulses,
- canned fish and meats,
- sauces,
- vinegars,
- oils,
- spices,
- dried herbs,
- preserved condiments,
- baking-crossover flours and supports.

### On-Demand Perishables

Bought for the week:

- fresh herbs,
- fresh vegetables,
- meat,
- fresh seafood,
- fresh citrus,
- short-life dairy,
- mushrooms and greens.

### Recipe-Specific Non-Perishables

Bought only if committing to a dish or cuisine direction:

- specialty sauces,
- unusual spices,
- preserved ingredients,
- dried mushrooms or seaweeds,
- cuisine-specific pastes.

## Candidate Recipe Test

A good weekly candidate should answer:

- Why does this fit the pantry now?
- What fresh ingredients make it come alive?
- What one-off purchases are required?
- Will leftovers be welcome?
- Is this low, medium, or high risk for a first cook?
- What would make it become a house recipe?

## Early High-Return Patterns

- Sardines or tuna with beans, grains, capers, olives, preserved lemon, and
  fresh herbs.
- Red lentils or split peas with bloomed cumin and mustard seeds, tamarind,
  coconut milk, and fresh ginger.
- Barley or freekeh stews with tomato, dried herbs, canned fish or beans, and
  fresh greens.
- Japanese curry rice or rice bowls using short-grain rice, miso, tsuyu, nori,
  bonito, canned fish, Spam, or on-demand vegetables.
- Chickpea or lentil dishes brightened with tahini, yogurt, lemon, mint, dill,
  parsley, or preserved lemon.
