## Cookware Safety Rule

- Stovetop cookware is pure stainless steel.
- Avoid Teflon and other nonstick-coated cookware because cockatiels are in the
  house and overheated nonstick fumes could be dangerous to them.
- Do not propose nonstick-dependent recipes or methods unless a safe
  stainless, cast-iron, glass, or oven-based alternative is provided.

## Fuel and Attention Rules

- Long unattended cooking is welcome when it uses the slow cooker, oven,
  microwave, rice cooker, or other suitable non-open-flame method.
- Long unattended gas stovetop cooking is not acceptable.
- Long continuous gas use should be avoided where practical because the house
  runs on gas bottles in a remote area.
- A `30` minute stovetop simmer can be acceptable when justified.
- A `2` to `3` hour gas stovetop simmer is highly undesirable.
- A common gas-saving pattern is to bring food to the boil, turn off or reduce
  the gas, let retained heat work, then repeat if needed.
