# Fridge Baseline

This file records refrigerated items that are part of the normal household
baseline or were reported as already on hand. It does not include ordinary
weekly perishables bought for a specific recipe.

## Stable Fridge Staples

| Item | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Butter | Fat | Cooking, baking crossover, sauces, and finishing. |
| Margarine | Fat | General use. |
| Greek yogurt | Staple dairy | Reported as a staple; useful for sauces, bowls, marinades, and baking crossover. |
| Red miso paste | Fermented paste | Reported as already on hand; store refrigerated after opening. |
| Kewpie mayonnaise | Condiment | Likely fridge item after opening. |
| Preserved lemons | Preserved condiment | Refrigerate if label or opening status requires it. |
| Capers | Preserved condiment | Refrigerate after opening. |
| Olives, black and green | Preserved condiment | Refrigerate after opening. |
| Minced garlic | Allium convenience item | Shelf/fridge status depends on product; track after label check. |

## Not Baseline Unless Planned

The following are normally on-demand weekly purchases rather than stock:

- fresh meat,
- fresh seafood,
- fresh herbs,
- fresh vegetables,
- fresh chillies,
- lemons and limes,
- spring onions,
- fresh ginger,
- mushrooms,
- leafy greens.
