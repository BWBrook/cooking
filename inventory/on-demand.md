# On-Demand Ingredients

This file records ingredients that are usually bought for a selected recipe or
week rather than treated as stock.

## Default On-Demand Perishables

| Ingredient group | Examples | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh herbs | Flat-leaf parsley, dill, coriander, mint | High impact for pulse, grain, fish, yogurt, and Mediterranean dishes. |
| Fresh citrus | Lemons, limes | Lemon juice is stocked, but fresh citrus is much better when a recipe depends on it. |
| Fresh aromatics | Brown onions, red onions, garlic bulbs, spring onions, fresh ginger | Buy according to the chosen recipe set. |
| Fresh vegetables | Carrots, celery, cabbage, mushrooms, leafy greens, capsicums, tomatoes | Not currently stock unless explicitly added. |
| Fresh chillies | Red/green chillies | Buy for recipes where dried chilli or hot sauce is not the right substitute. |
| Meat | Chicken, beef, lamb, pork, mince, sausages, bacon | Usually buy for selected recipes. In this repo, meat should generally be a high-impact garnish or accent unless the dish genuinely calls for it as the main bulk. |
| Fresh seafood | Fish fillets, prawns, mussels, squid | Canned fish is stock; fresh seafood is on-demand. |
| Short-life dairy | Cream, soft cheeses, specialty cheeses | Greek yogurt is stock; most other dairy is recipe-specific. |

## Fresh Items with Highest Expected Return

These are especially useful with the current pantry:

- fresh ginger,

## Planning Rule

When proposing a weekly recipe set, split the shopping list into:

- on-demand perishables,
- stock replenishment,
- recipe-specific non-perishables,
- optional specialty upgrades.

Default supermarket cadence is one weekly Woolworths or Coles delivery. Hill
Street Grocer can supply missing or higher-quality bits on Monday, Wednesday,
or Friday. Spice World is welcome as a dedicated specialty mission, but should
not be assumed as an ordinary weekly stop.
