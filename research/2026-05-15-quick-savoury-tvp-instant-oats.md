# Quick Savoury TVP Instant Oats

## Question

Can instant oats and textured vegetable protein make a very fast savoury
porridge that is more protein-friendly than a sweet dessert-style oatmeal?

## Short Answer

Yes. Use TVP as the savoury mince-like protein layer and instant oats as the
quick body. Rehydrate the TVP first with strong savoury seasoning, then add the
oats and microwave briefly. The bowl should take about five minutes and can
remain completely pantry-based.

## Sources

- Bob's Red Mill describes TVP as a defatted soy-flour product, a complete
  protein, and a vegetarian ground-meat substitute that takes on the flavour of
  the dish. Its published serving is `23 g`, with `80 kcal` and `12 g`
  protein.
- Quaker's oat preparation guide supports microwave oat cooking and gives an
  egg-oat protein bowl pattern in which egg is stirred into oats and microwaved.
  The same page notes that oats can be used with savoury toppings or stir-ins.
- Quaker Canada's savoury herb oatmeal recipe supports the broader pattern of
  instant oatmeal as a savoury base with herbs and egg.

## Adaptation Logic

The main problem with savoury instant oats is that they can taste like dessert
oatmeal with salt added. TVP solves that by adding chew and a mince-like
protein texture, but only if it is seasoned during rehydration. Rehydrating TVP
in plain water and seasoning later risks a bland soy centre.

The most pantry-compatible baseline is a gravy-herb profile using stock powder,
Vegemite or Marmite, onion powder, garlic powder, thyme or mixed herbs, pepper,
and optional nutritional yeast. This makes sense because TVP is already
recorded in the pantry as useful with miso, mushroom, tomato, spices, and umami
condiments.

## Formula Logic

For a one-bowl meal:

- `30 g` TVP gives a strong protein boost without dominating the oats.
- `40 g` instant oats gives a normal porridge base.
- `120 ml` boiling water hydrates the TVP.
- `150` to `180 ml` extra boiling water cooks the oats and adjusts texture.

The first trial should start with `30 g` TVP and move to `40 g` only if the
extra chew and protein are welcome.

Created `recipes/modern/quick-savoury-tvp-instant-oats.md` as a trial

## Open Questions for First Cook

- Is `30 g` TVP enough protein and texture, or should the house default be
  `40 g`?
- Does `150 ml` extra water make the bowl too thick after resting?
- Is the gravy-herb version the right default, or is miso-nori more appealing?
- Does the optional egg boost improve the bowl, or does TVP already do enough?
