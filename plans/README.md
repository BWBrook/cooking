# Plans

This directory stores weekly cooking plans.

A weekly plan connects:

- recipes or candidates,
- what is already in stock,
- perishables to buy,
- recipe-specific non-perishables to buy,
- cooking days,
- leftovers,
- and follow-up cook logs.

Use plans to decide what to order before the week begins. Do not treat a
planned purchase as a permanent stock item unless it belongs in `inventory/`.
