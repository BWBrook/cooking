# Quick TVP Cottage-Style Pie with Deb Mash

## Goal

Make a simple, relatively quick, robust two-meal cottage/shepherd's-pie style
dish using TVP instead of lamb mince and Deb instant mash instead of whole
potatoes.

This is best described as `cottage-style` rather than a strict shepherd's pie:
the form is the familiar savoury mince-and-gravy filling under a mashed potato
top, but the filling is TVP rather than lamb.

## Source Basis

This is a modern pantry adaptation of the cottage/shepherd's pie format:
savoury chopped/minced filling, vegetables and gravy, mashed potato topping,
then a short oven finish.

The recipe uses:

- TVP as the mince-like protein,
- Deb instant mash as the potato topping,
- gravy powder, tomato paste, stock powder, Worcestershire, and Vegemite or
  Marmite for a quick savoury base.

See `research/2026-05-18-quick-tvp-cottage-style-pie.md` for the source note.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to `200 C` fan-forced.
2. Choose a small Pyrex or casserole dish, roughly `1` to `1.2 L`.
3. In a bowl or jug, combine the TVP, boiling water, stock powder, Vegemite or
   Marmite, Worcestershire sauce, onion powder, garlic powder, thyme,
   rosemary, mustard powder, and pepper.
4. Stir well and let stand for `5` minutes while the TVP rehydrates.
5. Heat the olive oil in a stainless pan over medium heat. If using fresh
   onion, carrot, or mushrooms, cook them for `3` to `5` minutes. If not,
   continue straight to the tomato paste.
6. Add the tomato paste and cook for `30` to `60` seconds.
7. Add the rehydrated TVP mixture to the pan.
8. Stir in frozen peas or mixed vegetables if using.
9. Sprinkle in the gravy powder and stir well. Simmer for `4` to `6` minutes,
   until the filling is thick, glossy, and spoonable rather than wet.
10. Taste. Add a splash more water if too thick, or a pinch more gravy powder
    if loose.
11. Spoon the hot filling into the oven dish and level it.
12. Make the Deb mash in a separate bowl: pour in `300 ml` boiling water,
    stir in the Deb flakes, rest `30` seconds, then stir again. Add more hot
    water only if too stiff to spread.
13. Stir butter, margarine, or olive oil into the mash if using. Season with
    pepper and optional mustard powder or nutmeg.
14. Spread the mash over the filling. Rough up the top with a fork.
15. Add optional cheese or smoked paprika.
16. Bake for `12` to `18` minutes, until bubbling at the edges and lightly
    coloured on top.
17. Rest for `5` minutes before serving.

## Microwave-Only Shortcut

This loses the browned top but makes the dish very fast.

1. Rehydrate and season the TVP in a large microwave-safe bowl.
2. Stir in tomato paste, gravy powder, and frozen vegetables.
3. Microwave for `3` to `4` minutes, stirring once, until thick and hot.
4. Transfer to the serving dish, top with freshly made Deb mash, and microwave
   another `1` to `2` minutes.

Use the oven version first if possible; the browned potato top is doing real
comfort-food work.

## Timing

- Prep time: `10` minutes.
- Active time: `20` to `25` minutes.
- Inactive/elapsed time: `12` to `18` minutes baking plus `5` minutes rest.
- Total target: about `35` to `45` minutes.
- Make-ahead options: assemble earlier, refrigerate, then bake until hot. Add
  a little extra water to the filling if it has thickened heavily.

## Variations

- **More shepherd-ish:** use vegetable stock plus rosemary, thyme, black
  pepper, and a tiny pinch of mint; keep tomato paste modest.
- **More cottage-ish:** use beef stock, Worcestershire, Vegemite/Marmite, and
  cracked pepper.
- **More vegetable bulk:** add peas, corn, carrot, mushrooms, or lentils.
- **More protein:** increase TVP to `100 g`, add another `60` to `80 ml`
  water, and check seasoning.
- **Lower calorie:** omit oil, butter, and cheese; keep the fork-ruffled top
  and paprika for colour.
- **Richer comfort version:** add cheese to the mash top and a little butter
  or margarine to the Deb.
