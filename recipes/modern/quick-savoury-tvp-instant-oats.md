# Quick Savoury TVP Instant Oats

## Goal

Make a fast, savoury, high-protein instant-oat bowl that works as the practical
counterpart to sweet cinnamon/nutmeg/Splenda porridge.

The key move is to treat textured vegetable protein as the "mince" layer:
rehydrate it first with boiling water and strong savoury seasoning, then add
instant oats to make the bowl creamy and filling.

## Source Basis

This is a house pantry recipe, not a reconstruction. It draws on:

- TVP's role as a quick, lean, complete soy-protein meat substitute that takes
  on added flavour,
- standard microwave instant-oat technique,
- savoury oatmeal patterns using herbs, egg, cheese, or vegetable stir-ins.

See `research/2026-05-15-quick-savoury-tvp-instant-oats.md` for the source
note.

## Base Formula

Use this as the neutral structure, then choose one flavour module below.

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Textured vegetable protein | 30 g | Standard protein boost. Use `40 g` for a more mince-heavy bowl. |
| Boiling water | 120 ml | For rehydrating the TVP. Use broth if already made. |
| Plain instant oats | 40 g | Main porridge body. |
| Extra boiling water | 150 to 180 ml | Add with the oats. Use more for a looser bowl. |
| Salt or salty seasoning | to taste | Usually comes from stock powder, Vegemite, miso, soy, or tsuyu. |

## Baseline Flavour Module: Gravy-Herb Mince Oats

This is the first version to try. It leans into TVP's mince-like texture and
uses stock-only savoury pantry items.

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beef, chicken, or vegetable stock powder | 1/4 to 1/2 tsp | Start low; add more only if needed. |
| Vegemite or Marmite | 1/4 tsp | Strong umami and salt. Dissolve into the TVP water. |
| Onion powder | 1/4 tsp | Background savoury note. |
| Garlic powder | 1/4 tsp | Background savoury note. |
| Dried thyme or mixed herbs | 1/4 tsp | Keep the herb note small in a quick bowl. |
| Cracked black pepper | generous pinch | Finish with more if wanted. |
| Nutritional yeast | 1 tbsp, optional | Adds savoury body and a little extra protein. |

## Method

1. Put the TVP, stock powder, Vegemite or Marmite, onion powder, garlic powder,
   thyme, and pepper into a large microwave-safe bowl.
2. Add `120 ml` boiling water and stir well. Let stand for `2` minutes so the
   TVP rehydrates and absorbs the seasoning.
3. Stir in the instant oats and `150 ml` extra boiling water.
4. Microwave on high for `60` seconds.
5. Stir, then microwave another `30` to `60` seconds, until thick and hot.
6. Rest for `1` minute. Instant oats and TVP both continue absorbing liquid.
7. Stir in nutritional yeast if using.
8. Adjust with a splash of hot water if too thick, or a small pinch more stock
   powder/pepper if too bland.

## Optional Egg Boost

For a stronger protein bowl, add one egg.

1. After step `3`, beat `1` egg in a cup.
2. Stir it thoroughly into the oat-TVP mixture.
3. Microwave for `60` seconds, stir, then microwave in `20` to `30` second
   bursts until the egg is fully set.

This makes a thicker, softer, almost savoury-custard porridge. Use the larger
amount of extra water if adding egg.

## Batch and Nutrition Notes

- Target meals: `1`.
- Garmin entry strategy: enter oats and TVP by dry weight, then add any egg,
  oil, yogurt, nutritional yeast, cheese, miso, or condiments separately.
- Standard base rough estimate: `40 g` instant oats plus `30 g` TVP is roughly
  `250` to `270 kcal` and about `20` to `22 g` protein before optional
  flavour additions.
- Higher-protein base: use `40 g` TVP for roughly `25` to `27 g` protein
  before egg or nutritional yeast.
- Egg boost: add roughly `70 kcal` and `6 g` protein, depending on egg size.
- Sodium note: stock powder, Vegemite/Marmite, miso, soy, tsuyu, capers, and
  cheese can stack up. Use one main salty driver per bowl.

## Result Targets

- Texture: thick, spoonable, savoury porridge with TVP mince-like chew.
- Flavour: decisively savoury, not "sweet oats with salt".
- Protein feel: more satisfying than plain dessert oats.
- Speed: practical enough for an ordinary workday.

## Variations

- **Softer porridge:** use `180 ml` extra boiling water and rest longer.
- **Mince-heavy bowl:** increase TVP to `40 g` and total water by `40` to
  `60 ml`.
- **Lower-calorie bowl:** skip oil, cheese, and egg; lean on stock, herbs, and
  chilli.
- **More complete meal:** add frozen peas, leftover cooked vegetables, or an
  egg.
- **More heat:** add cayenne, habanero sauce, Aleppo pepper, or chilli flakes
  according to the chosen flavour module.
