# Tuna Pulse Pasta Bake

## Goal

Make a small, useful modern pantry bake from a back-of-can tuna pasta bake
recipe, adapted for pulse pasta, a `2 L` Pyrex dish, and the larger tuna and
corn cans actually available.

## Source Basis

Adapted from the recipe printed on a `420 g` can of condensed cream of
asparagus soup. The original used:

- `1 tbsp` oil,
- `1` onion,
- `420 g` condensed cream of asparagus soup,
- `425 g` tuna in springwater,
- `400 g` corn kernels,
- `250 g` dried penne,
- `1 cup` grated tasty cheese.

The user's planned substitutions are:

- olive oil,
- `500 g` tuna can,
- `420 g` corn can,
- Pyrex oven dish,
- pulse pasta penne,
- possibly slightly more pasta.

## Why It Fits This Pantry

- Stock strengths used: tuna, corn, condensed soup, pulse pasta, olive oil,
  dried herbs, pepper.
- On-demand ingredients needed: onion and grated tasty cheese.
- Specialty items needed: none.
- Equipment path: brief stovetop onion cook, then electric oven bake in Pyrex.
- Active-attention fit: low, with no long gas cooking.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to `180 C` fan-forced.
2. Grease a `2 L` Pyrex oven dish.
3. Cook the pulse penne in salted boiling water until just al dente, or about
   `1` to `2` minutes short of the packet time. Reserve `100 ml` pasta water,
   then drain well.
4. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a stainless frying pan over
   medium heat.
5. Add the onion and cook for about `3` to `5` minutes, stirring occasionally,
   until softened but not browned hard.
6. In a large bowl, mix the condensed soup with `60 ml` reserved pasta water or
   milk. Add more, up to `100 ml`, if it still looks very stiff.
7. Stir in the tuna, corn, cooked onion, pepper, dill, parsley, mustard powder,
   smoked paprika, and optional lemon juice or capers.
8. Fold in the cooked pulse penne gently. The mixture should look creamy before
   it goes into the oven; pulse pasta will absorb more moisture as it bakes.
9. Spoon into the greased Pyrex dish and level the top.
10. Sprinkle with grated tasty cheese.
11. Bake for `20` to `25` minutes, until bubbling at the edges and golden on
    top.
12. Rest for `5` minutes before serving.
13. Serve with steamed vegetables, salad, or a sharp green side.

## Timing

- Prep time: about `15` minutes.
- Active time: about `20` minutes.
- Inactive/elapsed time: `20` to `25` minutes baking, plus `5` minutes rest.
- Make-ahead options: assemble earlier in the day and bake when ready; add a
  small splash of water or milk if it has thickened in the fridge.

## Equipment and Fuel Notes

- Primary equipment: `2 L` Pyrex dish, saucepan, stainless frying pan, electric
  oven.
- Gas use: pasta boiling plus about `3` to `5` minutes for onion. No long
  simmer.
- Unattended cooking: oven bake is mostly unattended.
- Reheating method: microwave individual portions; add a splash of water if
  needed.

## Batch and Nutrition Notes

- Target meals: `3` to `4`, depending on portion size and sides.

## Result Targets

- Texture: creamy but sliceable/spoonable, not dry.
- Flavor: tuna-forward, gently creamy, lightly herbal, with a savoury cheese
  top.
- Appearance: golden cheese top with visible pasta, tuna, and corn.
- Leftovers: should keep well for `3` to `4` days refrigerated.

## Variations

- **Saucier version:** use only `250 g` dried pulse pasta and the full
  `100 ml` loosening liquid.
- **Denser meal-prep version:** use `300 g` dried pulse pasta, but only if the
  dish has enough headroom and the mixture is loosened well before baking.
- **Sharper tuna version:** add capers and lemon juice.
- **Greener version:** add peas, spinach, parsley, or dill.
- **Crunchier top:** mix cheese with breadcrumbs and a little olive oil.
- **Spicier pantry version:** add a pinch of cayenne or chipotle seasoning, but
  keep it light so it does not fight the asparagus soup.
